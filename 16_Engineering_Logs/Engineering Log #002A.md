## 4. Questionnaire Development

A stakeholder questionnaire was developed to collect structured information concerning:

- professional and operational context;
- current cold-chain monitoring practices;
- environmental condition monitoring;
- monitoring frequency;
- recording and storage of environmental data;
- accessibility of current and historical environmental records;
- challenges affecting cold-chain monitoring;
- potential information and system capabilities.

The questionnaire is being designed for industry professionals rather than university students.

The structure was informed by a reference questionnaire format reviewed during the mission, while the content was adapted to the specific research context of Kenya's horticultural industry.

---

## 5. Questionnaire Structure Developed

The preliminary questionnaire structure consists of:

### Section A — Professional & Operational Information

The section is intended to establish:

- respondent's current role;
- type of horticultural operation;
- area of professional responsibility;
- level of industry experience;
- involvement in cold-chain, produce handling, storage, transportation or traceability processes.

### Section B — Current Cold-Chain Monitoring Practices

The section is intended to establish:

- environmental conditions currently monitored;
- stages at which monitoring occurs;
- methods used to measure environmental conditions;
- monitoring frequency;
- methods used to record and store environmental readings;
- accessibility of current and historical readings;
- challenges affecting current monitoring;
- how abnormal environmental conditions are identified and communicated;
- usefulness of real-time environmental visibility;
- potentially useful monitoring capabilities;
- major cold-chain monitoring challenges.

---

## 6. Methodological Decision — Questionnaire Length

During development, it was identified that continuing to add questions independently to each section could result in an unnecessarily long questionnaire.

A decision was therefore made to target approximately **18 questions in the final questionnaire**, rather than retaining the initial 25-question draft.

The questionnaire will prioritize:

- relevance;
- concise wording;
- neutral questions;
- measurable responses;
- opportunities for respondents to provide additional information;
- direct relevance to requirements validation.

The questionnaire will therefore be consolidated before being deployed through Google Forms.

---

## 7. Methodological Principle

The fact-finding exercise will avoid leading participants toward the technologies already proposed in the project.

In particular, participants will not be asked to simply confirm whether they want:

- IoT;
- Blockchain;
- QR codes;
- Firebase;
- ESP32;
- or other specific implementation technologies.

Instead, the questionnaire and interview will first investigate:

> Current practices → Operational challenges → Information needs → Desired capabilities

Technology selection will subsequently be justified as part of the system analysis and design process.

This approach is intended to reduce confirmation bias and ensure that requirements are grounded in actual stakeholder evidence.
