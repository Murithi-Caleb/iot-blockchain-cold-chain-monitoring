## 1. Mission Context

Mission #002A was initiated to conduct primary industry fact-finding and validate/refine the preliminary requirements developed during Mission #002.

The preliminary requirements were derived from the approved project objectives, literature, initial scope analysis, and system understanding. However, before freezing the final requirements baseline and proceeding to Mission #003 — System Analysis and Architecture, stakeholder evidence is required.

The purpose of this mission is to establish:

> Kenyan horticultural operations → Actual operational practices and challenges → Information/system needs → Validated requirements → Final requirements baseline

Day 02 focused on preparing the primary data-collection instruments and preparing for the first stakeholder interview.

## 2. Activities Completed

### 2.1 Stakeholder Interview Preparation

A comprehensive semi-structured interview guide was prepared for the scheduled interview with a Production Manager from a horticultural operation.

The interview guide was structured around the following themes:

1. Professional role and responsibilities
2. Operational processes
3. Current cold-chain monitoring practices
4. Environmental monitoring and recording
5. Access to current and historical environmental information
6. Cold-chain monitoring challenges
7. Detection and communication of abnormal conditions
8. Produce batch identification
9. Produce traceability and movement records
10. Traceability information retrieval
11. Record management and verification
12. Historical record integrity
13. Desired improvements
14. Information requirements
15. Practical implementation constraints
16. Additional stakeholder observations

The guide uses a semi-structured approach consisting of core questions and optional probing questions.

This allows the interview to remain focused while giving the participant sufficient opportunity to describe actual professional experiences and identify issues not anticipated during preliminary requirements engineering.

---

## 3. Interview Consent Procedure

A participant consent script was prepared for use before the interview.

The consent procedure covers:

- purpose of the research;
- purpose of the interview;
- voluntary participation;
- right to decline individual questions;
- right to stop participation;
- avoidance of confidential or proprietary company information;
- intended academic use of the information;
- audio-recording permission, where applicable;
- confirmation that the participant is comfortable proceeding.

The consent procedure will be completed before substantive interview questions are asked.

No assumption will be made that consent to participate automatically constitutes consent to audio recording.

---

## 4. Interview Methodological Approach

The interview will use the following approach:

> Ask → Listen → Probe → Clarify → Record

The interview will not be conducted as a rigid questionnaire.

Follow-up questions will be used where clarification or additional operational detail is required.

Participants will be encouraged to describe actual practices and experiences rather than provide answers based on technologies proposed by the researcher.

---

## 5. Avoidance of Technology-Led Questions

A methodological decision was maintained during preparation of the interview guide.

The interview will not begin by asking participants whether they want or support specific technologies such as:

- IoT;
- Blockchain;
- QR codes;
- ESP32;
- Firebase;
- or other implementation technologies.

Instead, the fact-finding process will establish:

> Current practices → Operational challenges → Information needs → Desired capabilities

Technical solutions will subsequently be evaluated during system analysis and design.

This approach is intended to reduce researcher-induced bias and ensure that the final system requirements are supported by stakeholder evidence.

## 6. Stakeholder Questionnaire Finalization

The stakeholder questionnaire developed during Day 01 was reviewed and consolidated.

The final questionnaire is structured into five sections:

### Section A — Professional & Operational Information

Purpose:
- establish participant role;
- identify type of horticultural operation;
- establish area of responsibility;
- establish level of involvement in relevant processes.

### Section B — Current Cold-Chain Monitoring Practices

Purpose:
- identify environmental conditions monitored;
- identify monitoring stages and methods;
- establish monitoring frequency and storage of readings;
- assess accessibility of current and historical records;
- identify current monitoring challenges.

### Section C — Current Produce Traceability Practices

Purpose:
- establish batch/lot/consignment identification practices;
- identify information recorded during produce movement;
- understand the current traceability/retrieval process;
- identify traceability challenges.

### Section D — Records, Data Integrity & Information Management

Purpose:
- understand how records are managed and verified;
- assess the importance of reliable and verifiable historical records.

### Section E — System Needs, Improvements & Constraints

Purpose:
- identify useful system capabilities;
- identify practical implementation considerations;
- provide an opportunity for participants to identify additional requirements or challenges.

---

## 7. Questionnaire Design

The questionnaire was intentionally kept concise to reduce respondent burden while maintaining sufficient coverage of the research themes.

The final design targets approximately 18 question blocks.

The questionnaire uses a combination of:

- multiple-choice questions;
- checkbox questions;
- rating-scale questions;
- short-answer questions;
- paragraph/open-ended questions.

Where appropriate, respondents are given an "Other" option to allow information outside the predefined response categories to be captured.

---

## 8. Questionnaire Methodological Principles

The questionnaire was designed around the following principles:

### Neutrality

Questions investigate current practices and operational needs without assuming that a particular technology is required.

### Relevance

Questions are directly related to cold-chain monitoring, produce traceability, records, information management, and system needs.

### Measurability

Structured response options are used where quantitative analysis will be useful.

### Openness

Open-ended questions are retained to allow respondents to identify issues not anticipated by the researcher.

### Confidentiality

Respondents are not required to provide confidential, proprietary, financial, customer, or security-sensitive organizational information.

---

## 9. Relationship Between Questionnaire and Interview

The two instruments serve complementary purposes.

### Stakeholder Interviews

The semi-structured interviews will provide:

- detailed operational experiences;
- explanations of existing practices;
- examples of challenges;
- contextual information;
- unexpected issues;
- qualitative evidence.

### Stakeholder Questionnaire

The questionnaire will provide:

- broader stakeholder coverage;
- structured responses;
- frequencies;
- percentages;
- patterns across respondents;
- additional supporting evidence for requirements validation.

The two sources will subsequently be compared during analysis.
